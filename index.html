How SANDTAX works

This world has no built-in physics. Sand falls only because a law says SAND IS HEAVY. Delete that law and the sand hangs in the air. Every law is written in word tiles, and you can change any of them while the world runs.

Grammar

  • THING IS PROPERTY: LAVA IS HOT
  • THING IS THING: WATER IS SAND turns all water into sand
  • THING EATS THING: FIRE EATS PLANT turns plant that touches fire into fire
  • THING MAKES THING: LAVA MAKES SMOKE keeps producing smoke
  • THING HAS THING: ICE HAS WATER leaves water when ice is destroyed
  • AND joins words on either side. NOT cancels a property: SAND IS NOT HEAVY.
